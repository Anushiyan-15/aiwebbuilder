01 — About

Zenith Studio is a creative and digital agency shaping next-generation experiences for brands that intend to lead their category. Every engagement begins with the same question: what will this feel like in five years?

We work with a small number of partners at a time. That constraint is deliberate. It allows our designers, engineers and strategists to sit in the same room, hold the same standard, and move from first sketch to production release without translation loss.

Our clients are high-growth teams — venture-backed startups, scaled platforms, and established institutions rebuilding for a digital-first audience. They come to us for top-tier quality: interfaces that read clearly, systems that extend cleanly, and brands that carry weight in a crowded market.

“We design for the moment after launch — when beauty has to keep working.” Elena Marchetti — Creative Director, Zenith Studio

That philosophy turns into a method: research before decoration, systems before screens, and measurement before opinion. It is slower at the start and considerably faster everywhere else.

We are based at 450 Innovation Parkway in San Francisco and collaborate with teams across four continents.
